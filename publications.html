Research Output

Publications

Peer-reviewed journal articles, PhD thesis, and selected conference presentations in precision interferometry and space instrumentation.

Journal Articles

2023 · Physical Review Applied

2×10⁻¹³ fractional laser-frequency stability with a 7-cm unequal-arm Mach-Zehnder interferometer

V. Huarcaya et al.

Physical Review Applied, 20, 024078, 2023
doi:10.1103/PhysRevApplied.20.024078

Demonstrated world-record laser frequency stabilization using a compact Mach-Zehnder interferometer, achieving fractional stability of 2×10⁻¹³ — enabling future space-based gravitational wave detection.

2023 · Sensors

Single-Element Dual-Interferometer for Precision Inertial Sensing: Sub-Picometer Structural Stability and Performance as a Reference for Laser Frequency Stabilization

V. Huarcaya et al.

Sensors, 23(24):9758, 2023
doi:10.3390/s23249758

Novel interferometer architecture achieving sub-picometer stability while reducing system complexity through single-element design.

2019 · Classical and Quantum Gravity

Five degrees of freedom test mass readout via optical levers

V. Huarcaya et al.

Classical and Quantum Gravity, 37(2):025004, 2019
doi:10.1088/1361-6382/ab5c73

5-DOF optical readout system achieving 10× higher angular precision than commercial autocollimators at low frequencies, critical for gravitational reference sensors.

PhD Thesis

2024 · Leibniz Universität Hannover

Advancements in Optical Readout Technologies: Test Mass Sensing and Laser-Frequency Stabilization Techniques for Optical Compact Interferometry

Victor Huarcaya

PhD Thesis, Leibniz Universität Hannover, 2024
doi:10.15488/16938

Research conducted at Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute). The thesis presents novel approaches to precision interferometry for space applications, including world-record demonstrations of sub-picometer stability and advanced test mass readout techniques.
