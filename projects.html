WORLD RECORD 2017-2023

Sub-Picometer Interferometry

Precision at the Quantum Limit

Achieved the world's most stable compact interferometer — demonstrating structural stability below 1 picometer per root-Hz at frequencies below 1 millihertz. This represents the frontier of displacement measurement, limited primarily by fundamental physics.

Key Achievements

  • → World-record structural stability: <1 pm/√Hz below 1 mHz
  • → Demonstrated 2×10⁻¹³ fractional laser frequency stability with 7-cm interferometer
  • → Novel single-element dual-interferometer architecture reducing complexity

2019-2020

5-DOF Test Mass Readout

Optical Lever System

Developed a five degrees-of-freedom optical readout system for gravitational reference sensors, achieving 10× higher angular precision than commercial autocollimators at low frequencies — critical for future space-based gravitational wave detection.

Technical Highlights

  • → Simultaneous measurement of 3 translational + 2 angular degrees of freedom
  • → Achieved 10× improvement over commercial autocollimators below 1 Hz
  • → Differential wavefront sensing for angular readout
